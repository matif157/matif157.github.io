For business owners

You run a club, service firm, sales team or shop. Paper, registers and memory are stealing your money and your time.

Running on paper

  • End-of-day tallying is slow and error-prone
  • You can't see today's cash until someone writes it down
  • Unpaid sessions and "totally forgot" billing slip through
  • No reports — no idea which product, table or hour earns
  • One person holds all the knowledge. If they leave, the system breaks

Running on software I build

  • Live billing — the price calculates itself the second the service ends
  • Today's cash, sessions and expenses on one real-time screen
  • Payments tracked by method; WhatsApp reminders fetch the unpaid money
  • Daily reports and P&L at a glance — on your phone, anywhere
  • Roles and permissions; every change audited; backups automatic

Flagship · Live production

Asif Snooker Club — Club Management CRM

A snooker club in Faisalabad ran on a handwritten register. I replaced it with a system that runs the whole club every day — and it's the proof behind everything else on this page.

  • Live table view — every table's status and timer visible in one screen
  • Billing that calculates itself — hourly, frame and VIP rates, peak and night pricing
  • Payments by cash, JazzCash or card — receipts, invoices and daily closing in seconds
  • Bookings with deposits, plus WhatsApp reminders that bring people back
  • Reports and P&L the owner can check on a phone from anywhere
  • Staff got a picture-based Urdu guide — trained in days, not months

Custom PHP MVC, MySQL, Alpine.js, Chart.js — fully self-hosted (zero CDN dependency), tunnel-friendly, installer-based deployment, shipped with its own Urdu staff-training docs.

  • Clean MVC with PSR-4 autoloading, prepared statements everywhere, CSRF protection, RBAC with an editable permission matrix
  • Billing engine with rate bands, peak/night multipliers, minimum charges, discounts and partial payments
  • Configuration & installer — a non-developer can move it between servers and re-point the DB without touching code
  • Vendored JS/CSS assets (no internet dependency), tunnel-friendly URLs behind any reverse proxy
  • Docker-ready — anyone can run it in two commands, includes MySQL with persistence
  • Full SQL backup/restore, audit log, and 52 pages of picture-based Urdu/English guides

Under the hood

The senior-developer view: how systems are engineered, and where to verify it.

Data & reporting

MySQL with prepared statements, indexes for reporting queries, export pipelines (CSV/SQL) and print-ready reports.

Billing & payments engine

Rate bands, peak/night multipliers, minimums, partial payments, deposits, e-invoices and receipts — the logic lives in one engine, not in page code.

Security & permissions

CSRF tokens, throttling, RBAC with editable permission matrices, audit logging of every significant change.

Workflow automation

Event-driven triggers, scheduled reminders via cron, zero-cost WhatsApp via wa.me, broadcast center, follow-up recovery.

Self-host & deploy

No platform lock-in. Runs on ordinary shared hosting, Docker, or a $5 VPS. Installer + config screen so non-developers can operate it.

Documentation & training

Picture-based Urdu and English guides, 52 pages — proof that a non-technical team adopted the system in days.
